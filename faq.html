📅 Booking

How do I book a table?

Open Book a table, pick a date and an available time slot, and you'll get a reference straight away. You can check its status any time with your reference and email.

Can I order food when I book?

Yes. On the booking page use the Pre-order food box to choose dishes and quantities so they're ready when you arrive.

How many people can I book for?

1 to 12 guests. For bigger groups please send us a message on the Contact page.

Can I cancel or change a booking?

Send us a message with your reference and we'll update it. The status shows as cancelled once we do, and the slot is freed up.

🛒 Ordering and delivery

What is the difference between delivery, takeaway and dine-in?

Delivery brings the food to your address. Takeaway means you collect it from us. Dine-in means it's served at your table, and you can attach your booking reference.

What does delivery cost?

Delivery is ₹40, and free for orders of ₹500 or more. Takeaway and dine-in have no delivery fee.

Which coupons can I use?

FLAVOUR10 gives 10% off (up to ₹200). WELCOME50 gives ₹50 off orders above ₹300. Enter the code in your cart.

How can I track my order?

In the cart page, use Track an order and enter your order reference and email. The status updates as we prepare and deliver it.

How do I pay?

You pay on delivery or at the counter by cash, UPI or card. Online payment isn't set up yet.

🥗 Menu and diet

How do I find vegetarian dishes?

On the Menu page tick Veg only. Green, amber and red squares mark veg, egg and non-veg dishes, and chillies show the spice level.

I have allergies. What should I do?

Add your allergy in the notes when you order or book, and check the ingredient list on each dish page. Tell us before you order.

What can I cook with what I have at home?

Open the Ingredients page, tap what you have, and it lists dishes you can make, best matches first.

⭐ Reviews and account

How do I leave a review?

Open Kind Words, choose a star rating, write a few lines and post. You can review as often as you like. Add your order or booking reference to get a Verified badge.

Do I need an account?

No. Your name, email and phone are remembered in your own browser so forms fill in faster next time.

What are your opening hours?

Mon to Fri 12:00 to 22:00, Saturday 11:00 to 23:00, Sunday 11:00 to 21:00. Details are on the Contact page.
